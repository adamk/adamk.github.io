Why this series exists

The original $3k Account Series was a public proof of concept for the Foxchase discretionary 0DTE framework. The account grew from $3,000 to a peak above $22,000 before a significant drawdown reduced the ending balance to roughly $6,300.

One of the biggest lessons from Series I was discovering just how important 5-minute relative volume (rVol) is for filtering out false or lower-quality setups. Not accounting for low-participation conditions contributed heavily to the July drawdown.

August and September have provided time to study rVol further, reaffirm that observation, and tighten several other parts of the framework. At the same time, work has continued on the private algo and dashboard.

Before the discretionary $3k Small Account Series II began, the rest of September was used as an observation and development period. This included monitoring the market through the new Series II rules, addressing remaining algo bugs, and observing the gated Extrinsic Divergence trades recently added to the bot.

Series II asks a different question: can the same aggressive small-account growth approach produce a better equity curve when the lessons from the first series are incorporated from Day 1?

This remains a growth-mode experiment. Profits will be compounded rather than withdrawn, and position sizing will remain intentionally aggressive. The difference is greater selectivity, predefined risk, and more consistent execution. There is no arbitrary account target; the series will simply follow the rules, compound the account, and document what happens.

Series II Rules

  1. A+ setups: 50% deployment
  2. Qualified A setups: 30% deployment, primarily qualifying Extrinsic Divergences
  3. Anything below A: no trade
  4. Low rVol (5-min): no trade
  5. Default OCO: -20% stop / +20% target
  6. Stops may tighten, never widen
  7. Profit targets may extend
  8. No adding to positions after entry
  9. No chasing missed entries
  10. Re-entries must independently qualify as a new A or A+ setup

The operating principle is simple: take the qualified trade, size it according to the planner, define the risk before entry, never widen the loss, and do not manufacture another trade.

Setup Quality & Deployment Framework

Deployment follows current setup conviction: A+ is the highest-conviction qualified setup and maximum planner preset at 50% deployment (approximately 10% planned account risk); A uses 30% deployment (approximately 6% planned account risk), including qualified Extrinsic Divergence trades; below A means no trade.
