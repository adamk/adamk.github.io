Why this series exists

The original $3k Account Series was a public proof of concept for the Foxchase discretionary 0DTE framework. The account grew from $3,000 to a peak above $22,000 before a significant drawdown reduced the ending balance to roughly $6,300.

Series II asks a different question: can the same aggressive small-account growth approach produce a better equity curve when the lessons from the first series are incorporated from Day 1?

This remains a growth-mode experiment. Profits will be compounded rather than withdrawn, and position sizing will remain intentionally aggressive. The difference is greater selectivity, predefined risk, and more consistent execution.

Series II Rules

  • A+ setups: 50% position deployment.
  • Qualified A setups: 30% position deployment, primarily qualifying Extrinsic Divergence opportunities.
  • Anything below A: no trade.
  • Low relative volume: no trade, regardless of setup quality.
  • Position sizing is determined using the Foxchase 0DTE OCO Contract Sizer and the current account balance.
  • Every trade begins with a predefined 1:1 OCO order, normally using a -20% stop and +20% target.
  • Stops may be tightened after entry but may never be widened.
  • No averaging down on losing positions.
  • No chasing missed entries.
  • Re-entries must independently qualify as a new A or A+ setup.
  • The default 1:1 target should generally be respected rather than routinely converting winners into larger discretionary target attempts.

The operating principle is simple: take the qualified trade, size it according to the planner, define the risk before entry, never widen the loss, take the planned win, and do not manufacture another trade.

Setup Quality & Deployment Framework

Deployment follows current setup conviction: A+ is the highest-conviction qualified setup and maximum planner preset at 50% deployment (approximately 10% planned account risk); A uses 30% deployment (approximately 6% planned account risk), including qualified Extrinsic Divergence trades; below A means no trade.
