Series II Framework

What’s different in Series II

Margin-enabled account instead of cash account. This removes settled-cash restrictions so an early trade can be exited when wrong without blocking another qualified opportunity later in the session. Margin is not being used to intentionally increase option leverage.

Expanded trade qualification. The original Foxchase regime/z-score framework remains the foundation. Recurring SPX and NDX 0DTE GEX Profiles now contribute alongside named setups and other market context; qualification considers the complete opportunity, not a match to one named setup.

Setup Quality & Deployment

A/A+ are trade-quality, conviction, and deployment grades, not a closed list of named regime/z-score setups. Evaluate recurring SPX and NDX GEX Profiles alongside regime behavior, z-score structure, price action, key levels, relative volume, extrinsic divergences, and volatility behavior. GEX can inform potential paths, targets, rejection areas, and support/resistance structure. It is supporting context, not certainty or a standalone reason to enter.

  • A+ qualified trade: up to 50% deployment
  • A qualified trade: up to 30% deployment
  • Below A or insufficient confirmation: no trade
