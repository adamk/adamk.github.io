GEX Profiles

SPX & NDX 0DTE Gamma Exposure

The GEX Profiles library documents recurring SPX and NDX 0DTE gamma exposure structures. This first profile is an observed pattern under review, not a statistically validated result.

GEX may provide supporting context for an existing Foxchase setup, or it may form the primary thesis for a GEX-led setup. SPX and NDX are considered independently: some sessions may offer useful context in both indexes, in one index alone, or no actionable GEX interpretation.

Profiles may recur across different regimes and are not exclusive to a particular setup. A gamma level alone is not an entry signal. Any GEX-led setup requires confirmation from market behavior.

Profile 01 — NDX Negative-GEX Bounce → SPX Rejection

Observed in R1 Observed in R5

This sequence has appeared in both R1 and R5, making it a candidate cross-regime pattern. This is an observation, not a claim of statistical validation or predictive accuracy.

About these graphics: Profile concepts are hand-drawn in my notebook, then digitized with AI assistance for legibility. The observations and intended price path are mine.

  1. NDX begins with chop and balance.
  2. NDX declines to its middle large negative-GEX concentration and bounces.
  3. Price recovers toward the SPX top negative-GEX cluster, above a small positive-GEX nub, then rejects.

GEX levels describe potential structure, not certainty. Treat this profile as context and require price-action confirmation before any trade decision.
