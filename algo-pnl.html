Sequential sizing with capped overlap: New swing entries are skipped when aggregate contractual swing maximum risk would exceed 10% of current realized portfolio equity. Methodology and risk: Current rules are replayed chronologically without future information using validated ThetaData/NBBO inputs and true overlapping-position accounting. Historical performance does not guarantee future results. Large-account execution capacity may differ materially from this $10,000 research sizing.
