Modeled Alpaca SPY options fees and execution limits

Alpaca's published schedule generally lists $0 commissions for qualifying standard retail ETF-option flow, while pass-through regulatory and clearing fees still apply. The actual account classification, routing, partner terms and historical fee statements were not inspected. The historical model applies dated SEC and FINRA rates and uses the nearest retained Alpaca ORF/OCC/CAT schedule snapshot as an explicitly labeled retrospective approximation when an exact dated schedule is unavailable. Each fee type is aggregated by account and day and rounded up to cents.

Natural NBBO uses historical bid/ask leg quotes. Natural NBBO + $0.01 Slippage applies additional adverse slippage to the same historical trades and contract quantities; it changes execution prices, not trade selection. It does not simulate how lower equity could change later position sizing or order eligibility, and it is not a full live broker execution simulation. Modeled fees and slippage are not actual brokerage charges or executions. Historical quote prices do not guarantee complex-order fills, capacity or queue priority. This retrospective study does not establish a prospective trading edge.

Sources: Alpaca Brokerage Fee Schedule, SEC fee rates, and FINRA fee schedule.

    CORE benchmark methodology

    For historical comparisons, cash uses the U.S. Treasury’s daily 3-month par yield (BC_3MONTH). Annual yield percentage ÷ 100 ÷ 365 accrues and compounds by calendar day. Each day uses the latest prior-dated observation, carried for at most seven calendar days, including weekends and holidays. Longer gaps make cash and excess return unavailable; rates are not backfilled or substituted.

    Where the retained adjusted-price source has a close before the first period session, strategy, cash and adjusted SPY use the same $10,000 pre-session baseline at that close. The first plotted close includes the first session’s closed-trade strategy P&L, SPY return, and Treasury cash accrual. All series then run through the common validated September 21, 2026 endpoint. Cash is an accrual proxy, not an investable total-return index or guarantee of realized return; it excludes taxes, fees and Treasury price changes. Excess return is strategy cumulative return minus cash cumulative return, in percentage points.

    SPY uses Alpaca’s dividend- and split-adjusted SIP prices. The retained series begins on January 4, 2023 and has no preceding adjusted close for the full-history period; the page therefore omits its aligned cash and SPY benchmark returns rather than estimating or substituting a baseline.

    Sources: U.S. Treasury daily rates and Alpaca historical stock bars.
