Historical benchmark methodology

For historical comparisons, cash uses the U.S. Treasury’s daily 3-month par yield (BC_3MONTH). Annual yield percentage ÷ 100 ÷ 365 accrues and compounds by calendar day. Each day uses the latest prior-dated observation, carried for at most seven calendar days, including weekends and holidays. Longer gaps make cash and excess return unavailable; rates are not backfilled or substituted.

Historical comparisons use their own validated dates, with all indices starting at 100 and no rebasing. Cash is an accrual proxy, not an investable total-return index or guarantee of realized return; it excludes taxes, fees and Treasury price changes. Excess return is strategy cumulative return minus cash cumulative return, in percentage points.

SPY uses Alpaca’s dividend- and split-adjusted SIP prices, normalized to the first date’s close. Nontrading dates carry the prior session’s price. Missing required observations make the SPY comparison unavailable.

Sources: U.S. Treasury daily rates and Alpaca historical stock bars.

Backtest benchmark methodology

Uses the historical Treasury/cash methodology described above, independently aligned to the validated backtest period shown above, without rebasing. SPY retains the existing dividend- and split-adjusted historical series. Unsupported Treasury intervals leave cash and excess return unavailable.

The corrected Intraday result above is the authoritative combined replay. The retired prior portfolio attribution is not comparable and is not displayed.

Historical portfolio-value comparison

Hypothetical portfolios start at $10,000 with no deposits. Intraday includes modeled EX research; Multi-Day is standalone longer-duration research with daily marks. Intraday and Combined position sizes compound with modeled equity: gains and losses affect the size of subsequent trades. Later positions can be materially larger. These values are not actual account equity.

Combined is a single-account replay of current Intraday and Multi-Day research under shared capital and risk constraints, not a sum of independently compounded curves. It ends at the last jointly validated date.

Intraday excludes commissions, fees and additional slippage. Multi-Day includes modeled costs; Combined inherits each component’s treatment. Full broker fees, capacity and market impact are not modeled.
